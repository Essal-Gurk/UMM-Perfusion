# Third-party notices and provenance

This project is a clean native IsiXPluginKit port informed by the public
UMMPerfusion project. UMMPerfusion is distributed under the BSD 3-Clause
License; its repository also contains historical per-file BSD notices naming
the original contributors and Universitätsmedizin Mannheim.

Acknowledgement is due to the UMMPerfusion team at Universitätsmedizin
Mannheim / Medical Faculty Mannheim of Heidelberg University, including the
Computer-Assisted-Clinical-Medicine group. Historical source notices credit
Marcel Reich, Sven Kaiser, Markus Daab, Patrick Schülein and Engin Aslan.
The upstream README credits Steven Sourbron's Platform for Research in
Medical Imaging as the basis of its algorithms.

These acknowledgements imply no institutional endorsement. Upstream clinical
or regulatory statements apply to their specific historical product and do
not establish validation or authorization for UMMPerfusionIsiX.

- UMMPerfusion: https://github.com/Computer-Assisted-Clinical-Medicine/UMMPerfusion
- IsiX DICOM Viewer: https://github.com/ThalesMMS/IsiX-DICOM-Viewer-macOS
- IsiX plugin template: https://github.com/ThalesMMS/IsiXPluginTemplate
- Original licence: https://github.com/Computer-Assisted-Clinical-Medicine/UMMPerfusion/blob/main/LICENSE

The bundle links dynamically against IsiXPluginKit supplied by the IsiX host.
No GraphKit, OsiriXAPI, Horos framework, MPFIT or legacy binary from the
upstream repository is embedded.
